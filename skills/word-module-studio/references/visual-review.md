# Visual review checklist

Review every page of the actual LibreOffice PDF and PNG output. Record pass/fail/not-applicable
per item and page. An issue report is a completed review, not an accepted delivery.

1. Paper size, orientation and margins match the registered region.
2. Intended columns are visible and content stays within each column.
3. Section transitions retain preceding and following content.
4. No unintended blank page or excessive unexplained whitespace.
5. Titles and heading levels have the correct role and spacing.
6. Fonts actually present in PDF match the confirmed scheme or declared substitutions.
7. CJK, Latin and RTL glyphs are complete; reading direction is correct.
8. Body content, numbers and units match the approved source.
9. Inline mathematics has correct baselines and nearby punctuation.
10. Display formulas retain all operators, scripts, fractions, cases and matrices.
11. Single-line equation numbers share the baseline; multiline numbers align with the whole formula.
12. Long equations wrap safely, remain editable and preserve original mathematical structure.
13. Data tables fit their regions and retain every cell and merge.
14. Table borders, header repetition and row/page splitting are correct.
15. Every continued table segment has the configured title and numbering.
16. Figures retain aspect ratio, crop, position and relevant details.
17. Figure/table titles follow configured direction, numbering and attachment.
18. Charts, shapes and textboxes remain complete without collisions.
19. Headers, footers and page-number restarts are correct.
20. TOC/index/reference/footnote targets and refreshed page numbers are correct.
21. Comments, revisions, controls and unknown retained objects survive intended operations.

Also inspect page boundaries for clipping, overlap and number occlusion. Attach a receipt with
run ID, engine/input/template/output hashes, all page image hashes, reviewer and findings.
No receipt may be fabricated by the generator. Corrections follow the user's approved scope.
